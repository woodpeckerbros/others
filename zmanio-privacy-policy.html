Zmanio - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Zmanio ("the App") is a reminder and smart alarm application designed for Wear OS smartwatches, with optional companion functionality on Android phones.

We respect your privacy and are committed to minimizing the collection and use of personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Zmanio accesses, how it is processed, where it is stored, and how users can control it.

2. Information Processed by the App

Zmanio processes information necessary to provide its reminder, alarm, and synchronization features. Depending on the features enabled, this may include:

  • Reminder names, schedules, and settings.
  • Alarm times and smart alarm preferences.
  • Notification and snooze history.
  • Device status and notification-related information.
  • Location information for calculating religious times.
  • Heart rate and motion sensor information.
  • Locally stored backup and synchronization data.

App data is primarily processed and stored on the user's devices.

Zmanio does not operate a developer-controlled cloud service for collecting reminder content, location information, or sensor measurements.

3. Smart Alarm and Sensor Data

Zmanio includes an optional Smart Alarm feature designed to estimate suitable moments for waking the user within a selected alarm window.

When this feature is enabled, the App may access:

  • Heart rate measurements.
  • Accelerometer data.
  • Gyroscope data.
  • Motion and activity indicators.
  • Device-worn status, when available.

This information is used to estimate movement and physiological changes that may indicate a suitable waking opportunity.

Sensor information is processed locally on the smartwatch for the Smart Alarm feature.

Zmanio does not send heart rate measurements or motion sensor readings to developer-controlled servers or advertising services.

The Smart Alarm feature is intended for convenience and general wellness purposes only. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, monitor, or prevent medical conditions.

4. Location Information

Zmanio may request access to device location to calculate location-dependent religious times (zmanim), including reminders associated with sunrise, sunset, and other calculated times.

Location information is used for calculations on the user's device.

Zmanio does not transmit location information to developer-controlled servers.

Location access is subject to the permissions granted by the user through the operating system.

5. Reminder and Notification Data

Reminder content, schedules, alarm settings, notification preferences, and related history are stored locally on the user's devices.

This information is used to schedule reminders, deliver notifications, manage snooze behavior, and maintain reminder history.

Reminder content is not used for advertising, profiling, or behavioral tracking.

6. Device Synchronization

Zmanio may synchronize supported reminder data, settings, and related information between a Wear OS smartwatch and a paired Android phone.

Synchronization uses device-to-device communication technologies provided by the Android and Wear OS platforms.

Synchronized information is stored on the participating devices.

Zmanio does not maintain its own cloud synchronization database.

7. Backup and Restore

Zmanio may provide options to back up and restore reminders and application settings.

Backups created through these features are stored in locations selected or controlled by the user.

Users are responsible for protecting backups they create, export, or transfer.

Device operating systems may provide additional backup or restoration services governed by their own settings and privacy policies.

8. Purchases and Payments

If paid features are offered, purchases are processed through Google Play Billing.

Zmanio does not directly collect or store complete payment card information.

Google may process purchase and transaction information according to its own privacy policy.

The App may access purchase status and related entitlement information to activate purchased features.

9. Advertising and Analytics

Zmanio does not display advertisements.

The App does not integrate third-party advertising networks or developer-operated user behavior analytics services.

User information is not sold or used for targeted advertising.

10. Data Sharing

Zmanio does not sell personal information.

Reminder content, location information, and sensor measurements are not shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Information may be transferred between the user's paired devices when synchronization is enabled.

Platform services, such as Google Play Billing and operating-system backup services, may independently process information under their respective privacy policies.

11. Data Storage and Security

Zmanio relies on the security mechanisms provided by Android and Wear OS to protect locally stored application data.

We aim to minimize access to sensitive information and use it only when necessary for the application's functionality.

No electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

Reminders, preferences, and related application records generally remain on the relevant device until deleted by the user or removed through the operating system.

Users can remove locally stored application data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.

Deleting the App from one device does not necessarily delete information previously synchronized to another device.

Exported backups must be deleted separately from the locations where they were saved.

Zmanio does not maintain developer-controlled accounts containing users' reminder content or sensor information.

13. Children's Privacy

Zmanio is not specifically directed at children.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

14. Contact and Support

If you contact us by email, we may receive your email address and any information you voluntarily provide.

This information is used only to respond to your inquiry and provide support.

Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably necessary for those purposes, subject to applicable legal requirements.

The latest version will be published at this location with an updated revision date.
